## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of heavy industrial metallurgical supply chains and algorithmic high-frequency intelligence. Crafted for raw material procurement officers, metallurgy distribution executives, and algorithmic inventory operators, it unifies the visceral discipline of steel mills with the razor-sharp telemetry of high-density financial terminals.

The design movement combines **Modern Industrial Terminal Minimalism** with **Linear Precision**. 
- It treats data not as ornamental cards, but as mechanical instruments: calibrated tolerances, precise dimensioning, mill-spec metrics, and tight structural frames.
- Surfaces leverage deep carbon substrates reminiscent of rolled plate steel, hot-rolled billets, and graphite tooling.
- Accents deploy GF Industrial Crimson for authoritative actions and brand heritage, while trading engine signals provide unambiguous tactical directives (Buy / Hold / Risk).
- Emotional tone: Authoritative, uncompromisingly fast, mathematically exact, and operationally bulletproof.

## Colors

The system uses a dark-first terminal architecture calibrated for multi-monitor command posts and factory-floor tablets. 

### Color Strategy
- **Base Substrates (Carbon Series)**: 
  - `carbon-950` serves as the bedrock viewport canvas.
  - `carbon-900` forms the default pane and table container backdrop.
  - `carbon-800` is reserved for hovering states, card interiors, and active toolbar sections.
  - `carbon-700` establishes precise structural divisions and active selection backgrounds.
- **GF Heritage Carmine**: 
  - Direct translation of the GF brandmark. `carmine-core` and `carmine-dark` are reserved for primary terminal commits, high-priority triggers, and core telemetry alerts. Never diluted with soft pastels.
- **Titanium & Rolled Alloy (Secondary / Grayscale)**:
  - `titanium-steel` and `titanium-muted` render peripheral metadata, tabular grid alignments, axis lines, and low-priority telemetry.
  - `ice-white` delivers glare-free maximum contrast text against carbon backplates.
- **Predictive Engine Semantic Spectrum**:
  - `signal-buy` (#10B981) anchors bullish inventory acquisition and supply anticipation.
  - `signal-hold` (#F59E0B) flags price stagnation, neutral spreads, and spot-only purchase requirements.
  - `signal-risk` (#EF4444) drives sell signals, scrap dumping alerts, and margin warnings.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes information density and scanning speed under complex tabular pressure. 

- **UI Shell & Narrative (`Inter`)**: Controls visual hierarchy across page controls, analytical synthesis prose, and navigation ribbons. Letter tracking is slightly condensed (-0.015em) on headings to maximize horizontal yield.
- **Data Terminal & Coordinates (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular lining figures across all dimensions (e.g. `1200x3000x6.35mm`), financial quotes (PTAX, LME, CME), ticker deltas, nesting yield percentages, and predictive model confidence scores. Monospaced alignments guarantee that numeric columns never jump during live polling.
- **Micro-Labels**: Metric captions and system identifiers utilize `label-code` and `label-caps` in uppercase styling with deliberate 0.05em tracking for rapid scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The terminal layout operates on a compact, high-density 12-column modular grid with zero wasted breathing room.

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - Standard gutters default to `0.75rem` (12px), yielding maximum visual area for data tables and multi-series TradingView lightweight charts.
  - Multi-pane terminal layouts split between pinned left/top context panels (commodity tickers, quick nesting actions) and dynamic data viewports.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop (>= 1280px)`: Full 12-column terminal with docked order books, nesting previews, and candlestick engine.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1279px)`: 6-column adaptive view with collapsible sidebars and side-by-side metric tiles.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: Single column stacking with sticky buy/sell indicators and horizontal scroll matrices.
- **Density Scaling**: Padding follows 4px increments. Dense tabular cells use `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally to achieve maximum row density without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy blurs, nebulous drop-shadows, and decorative floating elevations. In their place, it establishes structural depth through **Tonal Layering and Precision Micro-Borders**.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - **Level 0 (Floor)**: `carbon-950` (#0B0D11) - Base background grid.
  - **Level 1 (Docked Containers)**: `carbon-900` (#11141A) - Data frames, charting canvases, and persistent sidebars.
  - **Level 2 (Active Cards & Toolbars)**: `carbon-800` (#181C24) - Interactive module panels and table row hover states.
  - **Level 3 (Overlays & Flyouts)**: `carbon-700` (#222733) - Command palettes, parameter dropdowns, and nesting optimization inspectors.
- **Micro-Border Engineering**:
  - Every card, pane, and separator relies on a razor-sharp 1px border styled with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or metallic steel `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.2)`.
  - Focused or active containers receive a dual micro-border: an inner 1px border tinted in `carmine-core` and an ultra-tight outline shadow: `0 0 0 1px #DC2626, 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Metallic Ambient Occlusion**:
  - Modals and floating sheets utilize pure technical shadows: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)` bounded by a 1px brushed steel border.

## Shapes

The geometry of this design system is strict, structural, and mechanical. 

- **Edge Definition**: 
  - Standard interactive elements (buttons, inputs, metric chips, and badges) feature a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This prevents the fragility of raw 0px corners while preserving the architectural feel of sheared steel plate.
  - Terminal windows, charts, and table panels feature a maximum radius of `0.375rem` (6px).
- **No Pill Geometries**: Organic capsules, circular avatars, and heavy radiuses are avoided, preserving an instrument-grade aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Industrial Action)**: Solid `carmine-core` (#DC2626) background with `ice-white` label. Flat surface with a 1px subtle top highlight `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`. Hover transitions instantly to `carmine-signal` (#EF4444); active press shifts to `carmine-deep` (#991B1B).
- **Secondary (Titanium Plate)**: Background `carbon-800` with 1px border in `titanium-steel` (opacity 30%). Text in `ice-white`. Hover deepens border opacity to 60%.
- **Signal Triggers (Engine Directives)**: Full-bleed semantic buttons for quick purchase orders (`signal-buy`) or scrap liquidation (`signal-risk`), with monospaced numerical shortcuts (`[F1]`, `[CMD+K]`) nested to the right.

### Metric Tiles & Signal Badges
- **Status Indicator Badges**: Monospaced font (`label-code`), framed in a 1px solid border matching the signal status. Example: Buy Signal badge uses `background: rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, `border: 1px solid #10B981`, and `text: #10B981`. Includes a pulsing 4px square status beacon.
- **KPI Tiles**: Built on `carbon-900` with top edge metadata (Material Grade, Ticker, Last Scan) in `titanium-muted` 11px uppercase, followed by `metric-display` values in tabular JetBrains Mono.

### Data Tables (Terminal Grid)
- Dense layout with row heights fixed at 32px or 36px.
- Table headers rendered in `carbon-950` with sticky horizontal borders, text in `label-caps` (`titanium-muted`).
- Alternating subtle zebra striping (`carbon-900` / `carbon-800` at 40% opacity).
- Numerical columns aligned right in `JetBrains Mono`; metallurgical product descriptions aligned left in `Inter`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `carbon-950` inset with a 1px border in `carbon-700`.
- Text typed in `ice-white` with monospaced formatters for dimensions (e.g. `Width [mm]`, `Weight [kg]`).
- Focus state instantly activates a 1px `carmine-core` ring with zero soft glow blur.

### Industrial Remnant & Nesting Cards
- Dedicated layout card highlighting steel remnants/offcuts. Features a split-view layout: left pane displays wireframe sheet utilization, right pane shows dimensional tolerances and QR asset tags with high-contrast barcode styling.
